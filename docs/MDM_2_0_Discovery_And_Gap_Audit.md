## 1. Executive Summary & Roadmap Reconciliation

With **`MDM-0`** (`6ce6165`) and **`MDM-1`** (`MDM-1.1` through `MDM-1.6`, accepted at `c1723711`) formally closed, Nexus3PL now possesses:
- Immutable global reference catalogs (`CountryProfile`, `AdministrativeDivision`, `TimeZoneProfile`, `CurrencyMaster`, `UomDefinition`, `UomConversion`, `BikramSambatCalendarMonth`),
- Issuing `LegalEntity` separation and facility geo-binding (`Facility.DefaultLegalEntityId`, `Facility.DefaultCurrencyCode`, `FacilityCalendar`, `HolidayCalendar`),
- DB-authoritative multi-instance configuration invalidation (`ConfigurationScopeEpoch`, `ConfigurationInvalidationEvent`, `ConfigurationConsistencyService`), and
- Fail-closed legacy country normalization, quarantine diagnostics, and guarded migration entry-point enforcement (`--migrate-db`).

A fresh, code-verified read-only audit of the `c1723711` baseline across `Domain`, `Application`, `Infrastructure`, `WebApi`, and `Nexus3PLFrontend` confirms four decisive findings for **`MDM-2`**:

1. **Rich Backend Foundations Already Exist — Reuse & Complete, Do Not Duplicate:** Core operational masters (`Facility`, `WarehouseZone`, `Location`, `LocationLock`, `DockDoor`, `PackagingMaterial`, `FacilityCalendar`, `HolidayCalendar`, `Client`, `ClientContact`, `ClientFacilityAccess`, `Customer`, `Supplier`, `Item`, `ItemBarcode`, `ItemCategory`, `ItemCategoryMapping`, `PackagingUnit`, `InventoryLot`, `InventorySerial`, `AllocationPolicy`, `PutawayStrategyRule`, `SlottingPolicy`, `ProductAffinityPolicy`, `InventoryAgingPolicy`, `AbcClassificationPolicy`) are already persisted in PostgreSQL. Missing admin screens do **not** mean the entities are absent.
2. **Severe CRUD, API, Security & Frontend Asymmetry:**
   - **Zero CRUD APIs or Admin UI** exist for `WarehouseZone`, `Customer`, `ClientContact` (post-create), `ClientFacilityAccess`, `ItemCategory`/`ItemCategoryMapping`, `PackagingUnit` (beyond `Each`), `AllocationPolicy`, `ProductAffinityPolicy`, and `PackagingMaterial` (write).
   - **Partial APIs (Create/List only, missing GetById/Update/Deactivate)** exist for `Location`, `DockDoor`, `Supplier`, `Item`, `PutawayStrategyRule`, and `SlottingPolicy`.
   - **Zero Admin UI** exists in `Nexus3PLFrontend` for every MDM-2 entity except `ClientsPage` (which only edits 5 scalar fields via `client.json`) and read-only `FacilitiesPage`.
3. **Confirmed Pre-Existing Security, Integrity & Policy-Drift Defects Uncovered by Code Audit:**
   - **Item Tenant Isolation Fail-Open Bug (`GetItemsQuery.cs:38-42`, `CreateItemCommand.cs:73`):** Non-staff callers can supply `request.ClientId` to override `_tenantProvider.TenantId`, and null `effectiveTenant` fails open in `GetItemsQuery`.
   - **Missing Facility-Scope Authorization (`LocationsController` / `DockDoorsController` handlers):** `CreateLocationCommand`, `BatchGenerateLocationsCommand`, `LockLocationCommand`, `GetDockDoorsQuery`, and `CreateDockDoorCommand` do not verify caller assigned-facility scope or cross-facility `ZoneId`/`ParentLocationId` ownership.
   - **Case-Sensitivity Uniqueness Bugs (`CreateItemCommand.cs:86-96`, `CreateSupplierCommand.cs:77-92`):** Both compare raw input before `.Trim().ToUpperInvariant()`.
   - **Policy & Safety Drift:** `AllocationPolicy.AllowMixedLots` is persisted in DB but never evaluated; temperature thresholds drift across `PutawaySafetyValidator` (`-18°C / 2..8°C / 15..25°C`), `AllocationCandidateFilter` (`-15°C / <=8°C`, ignores `TemperatureControlled`), and `SlottingOptimizationService` (`-15°C / 0..8°C`); `PutawaySafetyValidator` omits `Item.HazardClass` checks; and **zero operations policy entities** are wired into `MDM-1.5` `ConfigurationScopeMapper`.
4. **MDM-2 Scope Boundary vs. Downstream Roadmap:**
   - **`MDM-2` Owns:** Warehouse Master & Location Hierarchy (`MDM-2.1`), Client/Customer/Supplier/Access Masters (`MDM-2.2`), Item/Category/Packaging/Tracking Masters (`MDM-2.3`), Allocation/Putaway/Picking Policies + `MDM04-R05` (`MDM-2.4`), Wave/Replenishment/Cycle-Count Policies (`MDM-2.5`), ADR-007 v2 Metadata-Driven Administration (`MDM-2.6`), and Consolidated Security/Operational/UI Acceptance (`MDM-2.7`).
   - **Explicitly Deferred (Not in `MDM-2`):** Commercial Billing/RateCard/Invoice/Tax/VAT APIs & UI (`MDM-3`), Carrier/Service/ShippingZone/Fleet/VCTS Masters (`MDM-4`), External Integration Hub/KMS Vault/ERP Connectors (`MDM-5`), Consolidated Multi-Country Closure (`MDM-6`), and Deployment/Production Readiness (`Phase 8`).
